Scripts to cheese: from Broadway to Block Island to downtown Northport

RIND is the Northport storefront of Kaleigh Bernier — a Broadway stage manager turned charcuterie maker, who started curating cheese boards on Block Island during the pandemic and is now bringing the build-your-own counter to her new hometown.

The fix-it origin

Kaleigh is a Massachusetts native who studied theater in New York City and worked as a Broadway stage manager until the pandemic stopped the theaters cold. While waiting for Broadway to restart she was living on Block Island, Rhode Island — and found that the cheeses and cured meats she loved in the city were close to impossible to get on a small island. Her mother’s advice was blunt: if you care about it, then fix it.

She did. In 2021 she launched Block Island Charcuterie, building boards and elaborate grazing displays for weddings, private parties and other events — with marbled Sage Derby and butter-yellow Midnight Moon goat cheese among the staples — and grew it into the island’s charcuterie business. She still picks up substitute stage-manager shifts on Broadway (The Lost Boys and & Juliet among them), and the island business continues seasonally while the Northport shop opens.

Why Northport

The move came through her fiancé, Nick Graci — a Northport native she met in theater school, who now runs NorthStream, a Long Island video production company. Kaleigh wanted a year-round home for a seasonal island business, and Northport — waterfront, boating community, tight network of local shops — looked like the place she already loved.

“There’s just such a beautiful tie between Northport and Block Island,” she told Newsday. “All the yacht clubs go out to Block Island, so it kind of felt like a natural second place to open up.” And, to Greater Long Island: “I wanted to bring that piece of my home to my new home.”

The counter

The concept, in Kaleigh’s own words, is “Chipotle-style with a retail area”: a build-your-own bar where customers select the components of an individual or multi-serving cheese and charcuterie box, with a full shop alongside — local and international cheeses, cured meats, crackers, olives, salumi and specialty items a la carte. “The idea is that it’s grab-and-go, very quick,” she says. “They’re having a picnic, they want something to eat in the park, they’re going out on a boat, they can come in and grab something that’s delicious, beautiful, healthy.”

Ahead of the counter sit the preorder boards — bigger formats for holidays and events, custom boards and grazing tables — and on the cards: a cheese-of-the-month club and classes as the shop settles in.

The room

The shop is going into the Thompson Building: built in 1889 for the Edward Thompson Co., where enormous volumes of the American and English Encyclopedia of Law were printed until 1935, later a seat-belt factory, now restored office and retail space that keeps its original beams, brick and ironwork. The building’s owners hold tenants to the preserved brick and wood — which suits a shop built around beautiful, old-world provisions. Expect exposed brick and original beams over the counter when the doors open.

Questions people ask

When do you open?
Around Thanksgiving 2026. The exact day and the hours post here first — and people on the opening-day list get the email before anyone else.
What does build-your-own mean?
Founder Kaleigh Bernier describes the counter as Chipotle-style with a retail area: you pick your cheeses, cured meats and accouterments as you move down the bar, pick a size — individual box to multi-serving — and the box comes together in front of you. Grab-and-go, very quick.
Can I preorder boards for a party or the holidays?
Yes — that's the whole point of the preorder line. Bigger boards for holidays and special events, plus custom boards and grazing tables for larger celebrations. The details are here, and the form works for event inquiries too.
Where exactly are you?
The historic Thompson Building at 24 Woodbine Avenue in downtown Northport, with the entrance on Scudder Avenue — a short walk from Main Street, the village dock and Cow Harbor Park.
How much will boards cost?
Prices post here the week we open — nothing is guessed before it's real. What you can count on: local and international cheeses, cured meats, accouterments, and boxes built to your picks at the counter.
