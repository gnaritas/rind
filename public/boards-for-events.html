Boards · events

The big boards, preorder line

Not everything fits in a grab-and-go box. RIND takes preorders for larger charcuterie boards for holidays and special events — order ahead, pick up, walk out with the party handled.

Custom boards & grazing tables

For the bigger celebrations — weddings, showers, company parties, dockside gatherings — founder Kaleigh Bernier builds custom boards and full grazing tables, the same formats she built for weddings and private events on Block Island. The boards are curated, not assembled: cheeses and cured meats chosen for the crowd, accouterments arranged to be eaten, not admired.

Northport-sized occasions

The shop was sited for how this town eats: yacht-club get-togethers, tailgates on the water, Thanksgiving tables and holiday parties. Plans in the works include pairings with downtown neighbors — Sand City Brewing, a few blocks up Main Street — for food-and-drink events once the doors are open.
