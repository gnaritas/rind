The counter · how it works

Pick the cheese. Pick the meats. Grab the box.

One direction, one line, no guessing: the bar runs cheese first, then cured meats, then the good stuff on the side, then the size. Watch it come together and take it with you.

  1. Pick your cheesesLocal and international — a proper case, cut to order, from sharp aged wheels to soft bloomy rounds.
  2. Pick your cured meatsSalumi and charcuterie by the slice — the center of every good board.
  3. Pick the accoutermentsCrackers, olives, and whatever the case has that week — the figs, cornichons, sugared fruit and rosemary that make a board look like a still-life.
  4. Pick your sizeFrom a single-serving box to multi-serving boards for a crowd.

Grab-and-go, very quick

“The idea is that it’s grab-and-go, very quick,” founder Kaleigh Bernier says. “They’re having a picnic, they want something to eat in the park, they’re going out on a boat, they can come in and grab something that’s delicious, beautiful, healthy.” Downtown Northport is built for exactly that — Main Street runs downhill to the village dock, and the box is made to travel.

And if you just want to shop

The retail side carries the same goods a la carte: cut cheese, charcuterie, crackers, salumi, olives and specialty items — build a board at home or add to one you grabbed at the counter.
