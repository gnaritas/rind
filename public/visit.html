The room itself is the story

The Thompson Building went up in 1889 for the Edward Thompson Co., the publishing house that put Northport on the map: enormous volumes of the American and English Encyclopedia of Law came off presses in this building until 1935, shipped all over the world. It spent the 1950s making seat belts, sat empty for a decade, and came back restored — with the original beams, brick and ironwork kept, and tenants held to the preserved surfaces.

Other downtown neighbors in the building include a designer and a clothing brand; the cheese counter is the first food shop. And when the board is in hand, the walk is the point: Main Street leads down to Cow Harbor Park and the village dock, which is exactly where a build-your-own box is headed.
